I am a final year M.S. student at the School of Earth System Science, Tianjin University, advised by Prof. Jialei Zhu. I work on atmospheric chemistry and Earth system modeling: building emission inventories, implementing new atmospheric processes in CESM, and testing global simulations against observations.

My first author paper in JGR: Atmospheres (2026) presents a global model of atmospheric amines, covering their sources, sinks and atmospheric lifetimes. I am now developing a coupled new particle formation framework in CESM, linking amine and sulfuric acid chemistry to aerosol microphysics. I am currently a research intern at UNEP headquarters in Nairobi, working on air quality impact measurement.

For my PhD, I want to carry process based model development forward from aerosol formation to aerosol cloud interactions, and to combine physical climate models with machine learning.

Stage I · Global modeling of atmospheric amines

Sep 2024 – 2026 · Published in JGR: Atmospheres
  • Built a global emission inventory for methylamine (MMA), dimethylamine (DMA) and trimethylamine (TMA), combining six anthropogenic sectors with a new marine biogenic source based on air sea exchange.
  • Implemented the three amines as tracers in CESM 1.2.2 with the IMPACT aerosol module, adding OH oxidation, wet and dry deposition, and heterogeneous aerosol uptake.
  • Designed seven source attribution experiments and a source sink budget analysis; evaluated simulations against observations from urban, agricultural, forest, coastal, marine and polar sites.

Stage II · New particle formation in CESM

Feb 2026 – present · Manuscript in preparation
  • Adapted the CMAN framework from E3SM to CESM 1.2.2, writing Fortran interfaces between sulfur chemistry and aerosol microphysics (species and tracer mapping, unit conversion, array mapping).
  • Integrated amine and sulfuric acid nucleation, precursor consumption, particle number and mass allocation, coagulation and modal transfer; extended it to MMA, DMA and TMA nucleation using ACDC based parameterizations from the literature.
  • The coupled framework compiles and runs; current work validates mass and number conservation and process routing through diagnostics and controlled sensitivity simulations.
